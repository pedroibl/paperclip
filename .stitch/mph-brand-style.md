## 1. Brand Overview

### Brand Name
**Melbourne Print Hub**

### Tagline
> *Printed with Purpose. Delivered with Precision.*

### Positioning Statement
Melbourne Print Hub is Melbourne's go-to destination for premium, fast-turnaround print and design services — combining trade-quality output with a modern, creatively driven studio experience.

---

### Brand Personality

| Adjective | What It Means for MPH |
|:----------|:----------------------|
| **Confident** | We know our craft. We speak with authority, not arrogance. |
| **Creative** | We bring design sensibility to every print job, big or small. |
| **Precise** | Colour-matched, edge-trimmed, on time. Every time. |
| **Approachable** | No jargon. No friction. Just good people making great print. |
| **Grounded** | Melbourne-made, locally owned — honest, no-nonsense service. |

### Tone of Voice

MPH speaks like a trusted creative collaborator — knowledgeable but never condescending, enthusiastic but never over-the-top. The voice is:

- **Direct:** Get to the point. Respect the reader's time.
- **Warm:** Friendly and real. Avoid corporate stiffness.
- **Confident:** We don't hedge. We recommend and stand by it.
- **Local:** Subtly Melbourne-inflected. We're part of this city's creative fabric.

**Example phrases:**
- "We get it done right — and we get it done fast."
- "Your brand deserves better than generic. Let's talk print."
- "From Fitzroy to Footscray, we've got Melbourne covered."

## 3. Typography

### Design Intent

Typography communicates craft and precision. MPH uses a geometric sans-serif for headings (modern, structured) paired with a humanist sans-serif for body text (readable, warm). Both typefaces are available on Google Fonts.

---

### Primary Typeface — Headings

**[Syne](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Syne)**  
A geometric display typeface with distinctive character — confident, contemporary, and slightly edgy. Perfect for Melbourne's design-forward audience.

**Weights in use:**
- `700 Bold` — H1, H2, primary display
- `600 SemiBold` — H3, H4, section labels
- `400 Regular` — Large pull quotes (sparingly)

---

### Secondary Typeface — Body

**[Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)**  
A highly legible humanist sans-serif built for both screen and print reproduction. Neutral enough to support the expressive headings without competing.

**Weights in use:**
- `400 Regular` — Body text, captions, descriptions
- `500 Medium` — Navigation, button labels, metadata
- `600 SemiBold` — Inline emphasis, form labels, price points
- `700 Bold` — Alert text, promotional callouts

---

### Type Scale & Specifications

| Element | Typeface | Weight | Size | Line Height | Letter Spacing |
|:--------|:---------|:-------|:-----|:------------|:---------------|
| **H1** | Syne | 700 | 56px / 3.5rem | 1.1 | -0.02em |
| **H2** | Syne | 700 | 40px / 2.5rem | 1.15 | -0.015em |
| **H3** | Syne | 600 | 28px / 1.75rem | 1.2 | -0.01em |
| **H4** | Syne | 600 | 20px / 1.25rem | 1.3 | 0em |
| **Body Large** | Inter | 400 | 18px / 1.125rem | 1.7 | 0em |
| **Body** | Inter | 400 | 16px / 1rem | 1.65 | 0em |
| **Body Small** | Inter | 400 | 14px / 0.875rem | 1.6 | 0em |
| **Caption** | Inter | 400 | 12px / 0.75rem | 1.5 | 0.02em |
| **Button** | Inter | 600 | 15px / 0.9375rem | 1 | 0.04em |
| **Navigation** | Inter | 500 | 15px / 0.9375rem | 1 | 0.01em |
| **Quote** | Syne | 400 | 22px / 1.375rem | 1.5 | 0em |
| **Label / Tag** | Inter | 600 | 11px / 0.6875rem | 1 | 0.08em |

---

### Hierarchy Rules

1. **Never use more than 2 typeface levels in a single component.** Headings and body — that's it.
2. **H1 is reserved for page-level titles only.** Use H2 for section headings.
3. **Uppercase is for labels and tags only** (letter-spacing 0.08em+). Never uppercase body text or headings.
4. **Minimum body text size is 14px** on all digital surfaces. Print minimum is 8pt.
5. **Line length:** Target 60–75 characters per line for body text for optimal readability.
6. **Color contrast:** All body text must meet WCAG AA (4.5:1) on its background. Headings must meet 3:1.

### Logo Don'ts

| # | Don't | Why |
|:--|:------|:----|
| 1 | **Don't stretch or distort** the logo proportions | Breaks the geometric integrity of the mark |
| 2 | **Don't place on busy or low-contrast photography** without a background shield | Illegibility destroys brand trust |
| 3 | **Don't use unauthorised colors** — e.g., the icon in Studio Amber | Amber is a CTA color, not a logo color |
| 4 | **Don't add drop shadows, glows, or gradients** to the logo | Undermines the precision and cleanliness of the mark |
| 5 | **Don't use the wordmark alone at small sizes** — use the icon instead | The wordmark loses legibility below 80px |
| 6 | **Don't rotate** the logo for decorative effect | Rotation signals error, not creativity |
| 7 | **Don't place the logo inside a shape or container** (e.g., a circle or box) unless it's an officially approved badge lockup | Creates visual clutter and reduces authority |

## 5. Imagery & Photography Style

### Overall Aesthetic Direction

MPH imagery is **clean, craft-focused, and authentically Melbourne**. Photography should feel considered — like a well-composed Instagram post from a creative studio, not a stock photo library. The aesthetic balances the tactile reality of print production with the polished world of design.

**Mood board keywords:** Industrial warmth · Hands-on craft · Studio light · Melbourne lanes · Colour precision · Material texture

---

### Photography Style

**Subject Matter:**
- Close-up, macro shots of printed materials (embossed finishes, foil, paper grain)
- Hands holding or interacting with finished print products
- Flat-lay product arrangements on textured surfaces (concrete, raw timber, linen)
- Melbourne studio and workspace environments — natural, lived-in
- Designers and clients reviewing proofs in well-lit, creative spaces
- Street-level Melbourne contexts (laneways, café culture, retail) featuring print collateral

**Lighting:**
- Natural or softbox-diffused light as the primary source
- Warm, directional side-lighting to reveal texture and depth
- Avoid harsh flash, overly dramatic shadows, or overly bright studio-white setups
- Golden hour exteriors for lifestyle shots

**Color Grading:**
- Warm, slightly desaturated tones — complementary to the Off-White and Ink Black palette
- Mild contrast lifts. Avoid HDR, over-saturation, or heavy Instagram-style filters
- Skin tones should remain natural and warm
- Blues and ambers in the grading subtly reinforce the brand palette

**Composition:**
- Rule of thirds for subject placement
- Generous negative space — let the product breathe
- Mix portrait and landscape formats; maintain a healthy mix for social feeds
- Avoid overly symmetric compositions — a slight off-balance feels more authentic

---

### Illustration & Graphic Elements

**Line illustration style:**  
Simple, monoline geometric illustrations in Press Blue or Ink Black. Used to communicate process steps, iconography, and infographic data. Stroke weight: 1.5–2px at standard resolution.

**Texture overlays:**  
Subtle grain and halftone textures (referencing print production heritage) may be applied to section backgrounds at 5–15% opacity. Do not use on photography.

**Icons:**  
Consistent stroke-based icon set (e.g., Phosphor or Streamline). 24px grid, 1.5px stroke. Color: Concrete Grey (`#5E6470`) as default, Press Blue for active/hover states.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Typography

| # | Rule | DO | DON'T |
|:--|:-----|:---|:------|
| 1 | **Use the defined type scale** | "H2 at 40px Syne Bold for section titles" | Mix arbitrary font sizes or use Syne for body copy |
| 2 | **Maintain line-length discipline** | Keep body text to ~70 characters per line | Let text run full-width across large desktop viewports |
| 3 | **Uppercase for labels only** | "SAME-DAY PRINTING" as a badge label (Inter 600, 0.08em tracking) | "SHOP OUR RANGE" as a section heading in all-caps Syne |

---

### Color

| # | Rule | DO | DON'T |
|:--|:-----|:---|:------|
| 4 | **Use Press Blue as the dominant brand color** | Apply Press Blue to primary buttons, key headings, and section accents | Use Studio Amber as the dominant color — it's an accent, not the lead |
| 5 | **Respect the neutral-first approach** | Build pages primarily in Off-White and Charcoal; introduce brand colors with purpose | Flood a layout with Press Blue and Studio Amber simultaneously — it reads as loud and unresolved |
| 6 | **Ensure WCAG AA contrast on all text** | White text on Press Blue (`#1B3A6B`) — passes at 7.4:1 | Concrete Grey text on Off-White for body copy — borderline and unacceptable |

---

### Logo

| # | Rule | DO | DON'T |
|:--|:-----|:---|:------|
| 7 | **Maintain clear space** | Leave 1× icon-height of clear space around all logo lockups | Crowd the logo against a header edge, decorative rule, or competing graphic |
| 8 | **Use the reversed white version on dark backgrounds** | White logo on a Press Blue or photography hero banner | Use the full-color (Ink Black) logo on a dark hero image — it disappears |

---

### Layout & Imagery

| # | Rule | DO | DON'T |
|:--|:-----|:---|:------|
| 9 | **Let imagery and white space carry the premium feel** | Use large-format photography with generous padding and minimal text overlay | Pack layouts with competing elements, small images, and multiple competing CTAs |
| 10 | **Use stock photography as a last resort** | Commission or source authentic Melbourne-context lifestyle photography | Use generic, over-bright, American-looking stock that has no local resonance |

---

### Tone of Voice

| # | Rule | DO | DON'T |
|:--|:-----|:---|:------|
| 11 | **Be direct and specific** | "Same-day printing on business cards. Order before 10am." | "We offer a variety of fast printing solutions for your convenience." |
| 12 | **Sound human and local** | "We're a Melbourne studio. We get what local businesses need." | Use corporate-speak like "leveraging synergistic print solutions" |
